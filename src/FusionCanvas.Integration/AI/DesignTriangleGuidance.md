# Design Triangle Guidance

The design triangle consists of three corners that must reinforce each other:

- **Idea**: The emotion or familiar setting that the design communicates. This is the mandatory core of every design.
- **Phrase**: Optional on-product text such as a slogan, quote, or caption. When present, it should complement the idea and the graphic.
- **Graphic**: Optional visual elements such as illustrations, patterns, icons, or photographs. When present, it should reinforce the idea and accommodate the phrase.

A balanced triangle is one where all three corners work together toward a cohesive message.
